# Texture provenance

The six JPEG maps in this directory are derived from Poly Haven assets, released under CC0 1.0 Universal:

- `stone-*`: [Marble 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/marble_01)
- `wood-*`: [Wood Floor](https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_floor)

[Poly Haven license](https://polyhaven.com/license) · [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

Downloaded from the official `dl.polyhaven.org` asset host. Sources are the 1k JPEG Diffuse, OpenGL normal and Rough maps. Locally resized to at most 1024 pixels and recompressed for browser use. These are free public-domain assets, not proprietary museum content. Other small procedural maps and architectural geometry are original to this project.
