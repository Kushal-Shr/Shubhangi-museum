# Three-room celebration update

The previous five-wing design is replaced by three 14 × 14 metre rooms with 4.6 metre ceilings. Memories of Us and All About Her each contain exactly 15 photograph placeholders. The birthday room contains the cake, a letter and a film. The garden and entrance hall remain circulation areas.

The cake is substantially smaller and uses layered wedge geometry. Lighting, flame extinction, smoke, knife motion, separated slices, serving arcs and finite falling confetti follow server timestamps. The two configured character rigs have lighting, blowing, cutting and celebration gestures. A closer camera view follows the active celebration; the server requires proximity, correct action order and completion of the preceding animation.

Microphone blowing is opt-in. After browser permission, one second of quiet calibration establishes background volume. Sustained broadband sound above that baseline advances the celebration. Sensitivity is adjustable. This is an airflow-noise heuristic, not speech recognition: sustained loud noise can trigger it, and device noise processing may affect sensitivity. Audio never leaves the browser and is not recorded. A button fallback remains available. HTTPS or localhost is required for microphone access.

Validation covers the three-room routes, real Rapier collisions, reachable and non-overlapping photo frames, both avatar rigs, synthetic breath/silence/tone/transient signals, and real Colyseus clients sharing celebration timestamps. Multiplayer checks include rejected distant and premature actions, a late join during cutting, wall rejection and reconnection.

The browser automation connection timed out on this machine. Actual microphone input, visual animation quality and mobile touch behavior still require an on-device check. Historical verification documents describe earlier layouts and should not be read as validation of this revision.
